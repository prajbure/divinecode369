Privacy

Developed by ER PRAJWAL AMBURE. Updated 3 November 2026.

Account and device access

The owner approves Android devices through the website. The backend stores hashed session credentials, device names, expiration dates and approval records. Website authentication uses secure HttpOnly cookies and CSRF protection. Android session material is encrypted with an Android Keystore key. Provider secrets remain on the backend.

Saved information

Watchlists, alert rules and display preferences synchronize with the owner account. Android stores these user-owned records locally for offline access. Market and IPO feeds are not saved in the Android database. Private historical research follows the backend's separate retention configuration.

Notifications and external services

Notifications are optional. Android checks the authenticated backend inbox with constrained background work; delivery can be delayed by device power management. Firebase messaging, analytics and advertising SDKs are not configured. Backend market data uses Zerodha Kite and IPO Guru. Their terms and privacy policies apply when visiting their sites or the official registrar.

Permissions and control

Internet access supports data synchronization. Notification permission is requested only when enabled in Settings. The app does not collect PAN, bank credentials, OTPs, contacts or location. It does not place orders or apply for IPOs. Use Settings to sign out or revoke an approved device. Removing saved items or alert rules synchronizes their deletion. Clearing app storage removes local preferences, lists and encrypted session material; revocation on the website prevents further server access.

Hosting and retention

Provider resources use shared backend caches. Event inbox records expire after 30 days. Session records have fixed expiry. Server-side durability depends on the configured database volume; an ephemeral deployment may lose synchronized records after replacement. No data is sold. For account data requests, contact the application owner through the channel used to obtain private access.
